# Felipe Couto Duque

**Software Engineering — University of Brasília**
Databases and infrastructure

[Português](README.md) · English

---

I have been studying Software Engineering at UnB since 2025. I am interested in understanding how systems work from the inside, from data modeling to the infrastructure that puts an application online, and in how artificial intelligence can be used thoughtfully in software development.

I currently look after the database and authentication of **Agenda UnB**, for the Software Development Methods course, and the back end of **CarbonTrace**, in the Maker Foundation program at the UnB AI Lab.

I enjoy projects where technical decisions have to be justified, not just implemented.
